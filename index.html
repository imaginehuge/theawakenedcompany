About us

Built to give experts
their time back.

Too much of the world's expert time is spent on process: drafting, checking, formatting and chasing signatures. Our mission is to hand that work to AI agents that understand the rules of the field, so people can spend their time on judgement, relationships and the work only they can do.

Agentic by design. Our systems do not just answer questions. They plan, act and finish multi-step tasks end to end.

Deep, not wide. We go into one industry at a time and learn its documents, rules and workflows before we automate them.

Trust built in. Privacy, security and compliance are part of the architecture from the first line of code, not an add-on.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

The Awakened Company Private Limited ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal data when you visit theawakenedcompany.in or get in touch with us. We process personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under them.

1. Who we are

The Awakened Company Private Limited (CIN U62011UP2026PTC254466) is a company incorporated in India, with its registered office in Prabhu Town, Raebareli, Uttar Pradesh 229001, India. For the personal data described in this policy, we are the Data Fiduciary.

2. What we collect

  • Information you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, the contents of your message and any attachments.
  • Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record basic technical data, such as your IP address, browser type and the date and time of your visit, in server logs. These logs are used for security and to keep the website running.

This website has no forms or sign-ups. We do not use cookies, analytics tools, advertising trackers or social media plugins on it, and we do not ask for sensitive personal data.

3. How we use it

  • To read and reply to your message, and to follow up on it.
  • To consider partnership, startup program, investment, hiring or business requests you send us.
  • To keep this website secure and working properly.
  • To comply with our legal obligations.

When you email us, you voluntarily provide your personal data for that purpose, and we use it only for that purpose unless you tell us otherwise or the law requires something more.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run this website and our email, such as our hosting and email providers, who process it on our behalf, and with government or regulatory authorities when the law requires it. Some providers may store data on servers outside India. Any such transfer is made only as permitted under Indian law.

5. How long we keep it

We keep emails and the personal data in them only for as long as we need them for the purpose you contacted us about, and then delete them, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. Server logs are kept by our hosting provider for a limited period.

6. Security

We use reasonable security safeguards to protect personal data against unauthorised access, use, disclosure, loss or alteration. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to protect the data we hold.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you can:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • ask us to correct, complete, update or erase your personal data;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity; and
  • raise a grievance with us about how we handle your personal data.

To use any of these rights, email theawakenedcompany@gmail.com with the subject line "Privacy request". We will respond within the time allowed by law. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Children

This website is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Other websites

This website links to other websites, including the websites of our products. Those websites are covered by their own terms and privacy notices, not by this policy.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.

11. Contact and grievances

For questions, requests or grievances about this policy or your personal data, write to us at theawakenedcompany@gmail.com, or by post to The Awakened Company Private Limited, Prabhu Town, Raebareli, Uttar Pradesh 229001, India.
